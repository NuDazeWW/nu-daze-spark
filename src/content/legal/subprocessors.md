1. OUTSIDE SERVICES WE USE
These services process data to run SocialMotive.

2. HOSTING, DATABASE AND FILE STORAGE
Lovable Cloud. Stores workspace content, accounts and files.

3. AI GENERATION
Lovable AI Gateway, which routes requests to AI model providers. Receives the text and images you send for generation. It is not used to train models.

4. EMAIL
Your workspace's chosen email provider (SendGrid or SMTP). Receives recipient addresses and notification content.

5. PAYMENTS
Stripe. Receives billing contact and payment details. We never see full card numbers.

6. SOCIAL PLATFORMS
LinkedIn, Meta (Instagram and Facebook), X and YouTube, when you connect them. They receive the posts you choose to publish.

7. NON-PROFIT LOOKUP (DATA SOURCES)
ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API. Receives the EIN only. We keep the match result and ProPublica's citation, and nothing else from its records.
Internal Revenue Service (IRS). The original source of ProPublica's data (Exempt Organizations Business Master File and Form 990 extracts). We send it nothing.
