9. USAGE CAPS, ALERTS AND DEGRADATION
Your balance is checked before an action runs, never after. At eighty percent (80%) of a bucket you receive a notice. At one hundred percent (100%) that bucket is blocked, and we present the available unblock paths: purchase a top-up, upgrade your plan, add a managed allowance, connect your own provider key, or purge stored assets.
Where a single connected account consumes more than sixty percent (60%) of the instance's monthly credit allowance, admin confirmation is required to continue.
We monitor for unusual consumption on a rolling twenty-four-hour basis. Where a bucket consumes more than twenty percent (20%) of its monthly allowance in a day, exceeds three times the trailing seven-day daily average, or exceeds the published absolute daily threshold for your plan, we notify your administrator, pause that bucket, and require confirmation to resume.
When a cap is reached, the affected capability is degraded: AI generation halts, and where applicable X dispatch halts. Publishing to Meta, LinkedIn, YouTube and TikTok is never blocked by a cost cap.
Where you have an allowance but a third-party provider charges beyond it, we may pause the affected channel rather than incur unbudgeted cost. We will not spend beyond your plan limits without your confirmation.
