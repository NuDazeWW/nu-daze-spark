These Privacy and Data Processing terms form part of the SocialMotive User Agreement. They explain what information NuDaze Worldwide ("we", "us") collects when you use SocialMotive, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. ROLES
For content and personal data you or your users submit to your Workspace, you are the controller and we act as your processor. For account, billing and service-operation data about you as our customer, we act as controller.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
Account data: name, work email, password (stored only as a secure hash), role and organization.
Workspace content: briefs, drafts, approved posts, comments, brand kits, strategy rules, voice profiles, uploaded assets and files you connect from cloud libraries.
Connected account data: access tokens and basic profile details for social platforms, cloud storage and email providers you connect. Tokens are stored server-side and never exposed in the browser.
Usage data: feature usage, credit and allowance consumption, device and browser information, log data and error reports.
Billing data: plan, invoices and payment status. Card details are handled by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
Engagement data: post results you log or that connected platforms return.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
To provide, operate and secure the Service; to generate AI output you request; to meter usage and enforce plan limits; to process payments; to send service, security and notification emails; to provide support; to detect abuse and unusual consumption; and to comply with law. We use your engagement results and reviewer feedback only to improve outputs within your own Workspace.

4. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PROCESSING
Content you submit to an AI feature is sent to our AI processing providers only to produce the output you invoked. We do not sell your content and do not use it to train third-party foundation models.

5. SUB-PROCESSORS
We use sub-processors for cloud hosting, database and storage, AI processing, payments, and email delivery (including the email provider you connect). A current list is available on request at hello@nudaze.com. We require sub-processors to protect data under terms at least as protective as these.

6. SHARING
We do not sell personal data. We share data only with sub-processors, with platforms you instruct us to publish to, with your Workspace administrators, when required by law, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

7. SECURITY
We use row-level security, server-side role enforcement, private media storage with short-lived signed URLs, hashed reporting keys, and encrypted transmission. No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for managing your users and roles.

8. RETENTION AND DELETION
We retain Workspace data for the term of your subscription. After termination you have thirty (30) days to export approved content and media, after which we delete or de-identify Workspace data, except where retention is required by law, accounting obligations or a legal hold. Source video files are retained per the storage terms of the User Agreement.

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our sub-processors operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.
